# Alchemical Excellence

Endgame Nether alchemy for Minecraft Forge **1.16.5**.

## Progression

1. Mine **Alchemical Fragment Ore** in Netherrack. It drops Alchemical Fragments.
2. Craft nine fragments into an **Alchemical Shard**, or fill the **Binder** ring (ten fragments → one shard, ten shards → one Alchemical Block).
3. Craft the **Infuser**. Combine an uncharged **Mystical Stone** with a shard and press Infuse to produce a **Charged Mystical Stone**. The charged stone is a crafting catalyst and is returned after use.
4. Forge fireproof AE tools, armor, and the **AE Paxel** (pickaxe + axe + shovel with chance XP, earthquake, or full repair).
5. Hunt rare translucent **Nullite Ore**. Six Nullite Stones in the **Nullite Amplifier** become **Amplified Nullite Stone**, used to craft the **Null Multitool**.

**Chromaweed** grows in the Nether and is used for Pearlwood, Pearlstone, and related building blocks.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.16.5
- Forge 36.1.0+
